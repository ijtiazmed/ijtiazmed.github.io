Saudi vs Qatar vs Dubai: healthcare licensing exams compared

Last checked against official documents: 17 November 2026

Saudi Arabia's SCFHS exams for nursing, pharmacy and lab have 200 questions in two 120-minute parts with a scaled pass score (out of 800); its physiotherapy exam has 150 questions in 3 hours with a 60% pass mark. Qatar's DHP exams have 150 questions in 3 hours with a 50% pass mark (60% for pharmacists) and unlimited attempts. Dubai's DHA exams have 150 questions in 3 hours with a 50% pass mark (60% for pharmacists), cost USD 240, and allow 3 attempts at DHA. For nursing, physiotherapy and lab, all three countries test the same domains.

Is the content the same?

  • Nursing: Saudi Arabia, Qatar and Dubai use the same four domains. Saudi Arabia and Qatar also use almost the same weights (Adult Nursing about 40%).
  • Physiotherapy: the same 10 domains in all three. Saudi Arabia and Qatar use identical weights, and Dubai lists Qatar's reference books plus one more.
  • Laboratory: the same 9 domains in all three, from Blood Bank to Patient Safety and Professionalism.
  • Pharmacy: this is where they differ most. Qatar's exam is much more clinical (67 of 150 questions) and uses Qatar Ministry of Public Health documents. Dubai lists 14 content areas with no weights. Saudi Arabia's SPLE includes pharmacy law and Saudi healthcare organisations.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use the same preparation for Saudi, Qatar and Dubai?

For nursing, physiotherapy and lab, mostly yes: the three countries test the same domains, and only the format, timing and pass mark differ. Leave out country-specific law questions for the countries you're not sitting. Pharmacy needs more country-specific work.

Which is harder, the DHA or the SCFHS exam?

There is no official evidence either way: we found no published pass rates for either exam. For nursing, physiotherapy and lab, DHA covers the same domains as Saudi Arabia and Qatar. DHA can feel harder because you get only 3 attempts and no score, just pass or fail.

Which exam has the most attempts?

Qatar's DHP: its exam page now allows unlimited attempts. Saudi Arabia's SNLE and SPLE: up to 4 a year until you pass. Dubai's DHA: 3, then possibly 1 final attempt with a different UAE authority.
