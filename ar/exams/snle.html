دليل اختبار SNLE للتمريض في السعودية

آخر مراجعة مع الوثائق الرسمية: 17 نوفمبر 2026

اختبار SNLE هو اختبار الترخيص للممرضين لدى الهيئة السعودية للتخصصات الصحية. يتكوّن من 200 سؤال اختيار من متعدد في جزأين، كل جزء 100 سؤال ومدته 120 دقيقة. درجة النجاح 500 على مقياس موزون من 200 إلى 800، ويمكنك أداؤه حتى 4 مرات في السنة إلى أن تنجح. أكبر مجال هو تمريض البالغين (40%)، ثم تمريض الأم والطفل (30%)، ثم أساسيات التمريض (20%)، ثم الإدارة والقيادة (10%).

Question 1

A Saudi court formally asks a hospital for the medical records of a patient involved in a legal case. The patient has not given consent. How should the request be viewed under the Saudi code of ethics for healthcare practitioners?

  1. Allowed only if the patient's lawyer collects the records
  2. Allowed only if the patient has since died
  3. Allowed, as a request from a judicial authority is an exception
  4. Refused, as records can never be shared without written consent
أظهر الإجابة

C. Allowed, as a request from a judicial authority is an exception The code allows disclosure without consent in a small number of situations that serve a major public interest. A request from a judicial authority is one of them, and the information goes only to that official authority. Saying records can never be shared ignores these listed exceptions. The patient's death and collection by a lawyer are not conditions the code sets for this exception.

Source: SCFHS – Code of Ethics for Healthcare Practitioners, 3rd ed. (2014)

Question 2

A 7-year-old is admitted with mumps; the parotid swelling began yesterday. Which precautions are needed, and for how long?

  1. Contact, until the swelling has fully gone down
  2. Droplet, until 5 days after swelling began
  3. Droplet, until 24 hours of antibiotics
  4. Airborne, until the fever settles
أظهر الإجابة

B. Droplet, until 5 days after swelling began CDC lists mumps under droplet plus standard precautions until 5 days after the onset of swelling. Mumps is a virus spread by respiratory droplets, so airborne and contact precautions are not the correct type. Antibiotics do not treat mumps, so a 24-hour antibiotic rule does not apply; that duration is used for meningococcal disease.

Source: CDC – Guideline for Isolation Precautions (2007), Appendix A: Type and Duration of Precautions

Question 3

A malnourished patient starting isoniazid has diabetes and drinks alcohol heavily. Which supplement should be given to prevent a common side effect?

  1. Calcium
  2. Vitamin K (phytomenadione)
  3. Pyridoxine (vitamin B6)
  4. Folic acid
أظهر الإجابة

C. Pyridoxine (vitamin B6) The isoniazid label recommends giving pyridoxine (vitamin B6) to people who are malnourished or predisposed to neuropathy, such as those with diabetes or alcohol use disorder. Isoniazid interferes with B6 and can cause peripheral neuropathy. Vitamin K, folic acid and calcium do not prevent this effect.

Source: Isoniazid tablets (Chartwell) prescribing information – DailyMed

Question 4

A 68-year-old man's serum creatinine was 90 µmol/L on admission. Thirty-six hours later it is 120 µmol/L. Does this meet a criterion for acute kidney injury?

  1. Only if eGFR has also fallen below 15
  2. No: creatinine must at least double first
  3. Yes: a rise of ≥26 µmol/L within 48 hours
  4. No: only urine output can define AKI
أظهر الإجابة

C. Yes: a rise of ≥26 µmol/L within 48 hours A rise in serum creatinine of 26 µmol/L or more within 48 hours is one criterion for detecting AKI. This patient rose by 30 µmol/L in 36 hours. Other criteria include a 50% rise within 7 days, or urine output below 0.5 mL/kg/hour for more than 6 hours. A doubling of creatinine is not required. Urine output is only one possible criterion. An eGFR below 15 describes kidney failure, not the detection of AKI.

Source: NICE NG148 – Acute kidney injury

Question 5

On day 3 after his last drink, a patient becomes disoriented, sees insects on the walls and has coarse tremor and a fever. What is the first-line treatment?

  1. Oral haloperidol alone
  2. Oral disulfiram
  3. Oral lorazepam
  4. IV morphine
أظهر الإجابة

C. Oral lorazepam Disorientation, visual hallucinations, tremor and fever a few days after stopping alcohol describe delirium tremens. NICE recommends oral lorazepam as first-line treatment. Haloperidol alone is not first-line and does not prevent withdrawal seizures. Morphine does not treat withdrawal, and disulfiram is used to support abstinence later, never during acute withdrawal.

Source: NICE CG100 – Alcohol-use disorders: diagnosis and management of physical complications (2010, updated 2017)

Question 6

A patient with depression asks whether anything really helps. What should the nurse tell him?

  1. Psychological treatment and medicines are effective
  2. Vitamins alone cure depression
  3. Only hospital admission helps
  4. There are no treatments that work for depression
أظهر الإجابة

A. Psychological treatment and medicines are effective WHO states that there are effective treatments for depression, including psychological treatment and medicines. Psychological treatments are first-line and can be combined with antidepressants for moderate and severe depression. Most people are treated in the community, not hospital, and vitamins alone are not a cure.

Source: WHO – Depressive disorder (depression) fact sheet

Question 7

A client with severe hypocalcaemia after thyroid surgery has tingling around the mouth. Which new finding is the nurse's highest priority?

  1. Noisy, high-pitched breathing (stridor)
  2. Muscle cramps in both calves
  3. Numbness and tingling in the fingers
  4. Positive Chvostek's sign on the cheek
أظهر الإجابة

A. Noisy, high-pitched breathing (stridor) All four are signs of low calcium, but stridor suggests laryngospasm, which can rapidly block the airway and is life-threatening. Using the ABCs, the airway problem is addressed first, with urgent calcium replacement. Finger numbness, muscle cramps and a positive Chvostek's sign are important and are reported, but they do not threaten the airway immediately.

Source: Open RN – Health Alterations (2024), 7.8 Parathyroid Disorders

Question 8

A client has frequent diarrhoea with blood and mucus, and colonoscopy shows continuous inflammation limited to the colon. Which condition is most likely?

  1. Crohn's disease
  2. Ulcerative colitis
  3. Irritable bowel syndrome
  4. Diverticulitis
أظهر الإجابة

B. Ulcerative colitis Ulcerative colitis causes continuous ulceration confined to the large intestine, with bloody diarrhoea containing mucus. Crohn's disease can affect any part of the GI tract with patchy skip lesions and usually non-bloody diarrhoea. Irritable bowel syndrome does not cause inflammation or bleeding. Diverticulitis causes left lower quadrant pain and fever rather than continuous colitis.

Source: Open RN – Health Alterations (2024), 11.11 Inflammatory Bowel Disease

Question 9

A client on phenytoin develops a new skin rash. What should the nurse do?

  1. Double the dose to control seizures
  2. Give the dose and recheck the rash tomorrow
  3. Apply lotion and continue as normal
  4. Hold the next dose and notify the prescriber
أظهر الإجابة

D. Hold the next dose and notify the prescriber A rash can be the first sign of a serious skin reaction to phenytoin, and the drug should be discontinued at the first sign of a rash. The nurse holds the next dose and contacts the prescriber promptly. Because abrupt withdrawal can trigger seizures, the prescriber will usually arrange a substitute anticonvulsant. Continuing or doubling the dose is unsafe.

Source: Open RN – Nursing Pharmacology 2e, 8.10 Anticonvulsants

Question 10

A 9-month-old breastfed baby has gastroenteritis without signs of dehydration. What should the nurse advise about feeding?

  1. Stop breastfeeding until the diarrhoea settles
  2. Switch to diluted formula
  3. Continue breastfeeding and other milk feeds
  4. Give only water for 24 hours
أظهر الإجابة

C. Continue breastfeeding and other milk feeds For children with gastroenteritis who are not dehydrated, NICE advises continuing breastfeeding and other milk feeds. Breast milk provides fluid and nutrition and does not need to be stopped. Giving only water or diluted formula reduces nutrition without helping the illness, and stopping breastfeeding can make dehydration more likely.

Source: NICE CG84 – Diarrhoea and vomiting caused by gastroenteritis in under 5s (2009)

Question 11

Which of these is a CDC milestone for a child at 18 months?

  1. Says 3+ words besides 'mama' or 'dada'
  2. Hops on one foot
  3. Writes all the letters of his own name
  4. Talks in full sentences of 6 words
أظهر الإجابة

A. Says 3+ words besides 'mama' or 'dada' CDC lists trying to say three or more words besides 'mama' or 'dada' as an 18-month milestone, along with pointing to show something interesting and copying chores. Writing letters, speaking in long sentences and hopping on one foot are later skills, so they are not expected at 18 months.

Source: CDC – Important Milestones: Your Child By Eighteen Months

Question 12

A neonate is receiving IV ceftriaxone. A prescription for IV calcium gluconate is added. What risk does the ceftriaxone label warn about in neonates?

  1. Ceftriaxone-calcium precipitation
  2. Severe hypoglycaemia in the first day
  3. Tooth discolouration
  4. Hearing loss
أظهر الإجابة

A. Ceftriaxone-calcium precipitation The ceftriaxone label warns that neonates have an increased risk of precipitation of ceftriaxone-calcium, which can deposit in the lungs and kidneys. Ceftriaxone and calcium-containing IV solutions should not be given together in neonates, and the prescriber should be contacted. Hypoglycaemia, hearing loss and tooth staining are not the specific warning here.

Source: Ceftriaxone for Injection (Samson) prescribing information – DailyMed

Question 13

Under the Saudi national immunisation schedule, when are the two doses of chickenpox (varicella) vaccine given?

  1. 12 months and 18 months
  2. 18 months and 4–6 years
  3. 12 months and 4–6 years
  4. 9 months and 18 months
أظهر الإجابة

B. 18 months and 4–6 years In the Saudi schedule, varicella vaccine is given as a separate vaccine at 18 months and again at 4–6 years, the same visits as the second and third MMR doses. It is not given at 12 months in this schedule, although some other countries start it then. The 9-month visit gives single measles vaccine, and varicella, a live vaccine, is not given routinely before 12 months.

Source: Saudi Ministry of Health – National Immunization Schedule (PDF, 2019 edition; the current schedule linked from the MOH Immunization Schedule page, last updated 2026)

Question 14

A quality nurse is presenting WHO patient safety data to staff. About what share of preventable patient harm does WHO attribute to medications?

  1. About half
  2. Almost all
  3. About one-tenth
  4. About one-quarter
أظهر الإجابة

A. About half WHO estimates that around 1 in 10 patients is harmed in health care, that over half of this harm is preventable, and that about half of the preventable harm comes from medications. This is why medication safety, including high-alert drugs, double checks and reconciliation, is a top priority. One-tenth and one-quarter understate the medication share, and 'almost all' overstates it.

Source: WHO – Patient safety fact sheet (2023)

Question 15

A client has several nursing diagnoses. Which should the nurse address first?

  1. Risk for falls
  2. Acute pain occurring now
  3. Risk for constipation
  4. Readiness for enhanced knowledge
أظهر الإجابة

B. Acute pain occurring now Actual problems that are actively occurring generally take priority over potential (risk) problems, provided airway, breathing and circulation are stable. The client's current acute pain needs attention now. Risk for falls and constipation are important but have not yet occurred and are managed with preventive measures. A health-promotion diagnosis has the lowest urgency.

Source: Open RN – Health Alterations (2024), 1.9 Prioritization Strategies

كم مرة يمكنني أداء اختبار SNLE؟

حتى 4 مرات في السنة، تُحسب من أول محاولة إلى أن تنجح. لا يُسمح بأداء الاختبار مرتين في الفترة الاختبارية نفسها، والمحاولة الثانية تُلغى وتُحتسب محاولة.

هل يمكنني أداء اختبار SNLE من المنزل؟

لا. يُؤدّى الاختبار الحقيقي في مركز Prometric، أما الخيار عن بُعد لدى Prometric فهو للاختبارات التجريبية فقط.
