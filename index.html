Written by Ijtiaz Med for practice, from the app's free starter set. These are not questions from the real exam.

After the baby's head is born, the shoulders do not deliver with normal traction. Which manoeuvre is usually tried first?

  1. Pushing the head back in
  2. McRoberts manoeuvre
  3. Fundal pressure
  4. Leopold's manoeuvres
Show answer

B. McRoberts manoeuvre This is shoulder dystocia, an emergency with no reliable warning signs. The first manoeuvre is McRoberts: the woman's legs are flexed sharply so her thighs press against her abdomen, which widens the pelvic outlet. Fundal pressure can worsen impaction and is avoided. Leopold's manoeuvres are abdominal palpations done in pregnancy, not a delivery manoeuvre. Pushing the head back is a last-resort measure.

Source: Open RN – Nursing: Health Promotion, 10.8 Complications and Medical Interventions During the Second Stage of Labor

Frequently asked questions

What is Ijtiaz Med?

Ijtiaz Med is a free exam-prep app for healthcare licensing exams in the Gulf. It has more than 6,000 practice questions for nurses, pharmacists, physiotherapists and laboratory staff preparing for the Saudi (SCFHS), Qatar (DHP) and Dubai (DHA) exams.

Is Ijtiaz Med free?

Yes, the app is free at launch.

Which exams does Ijtiaz Med cover?

The SNLE, SPLE, SLLE and physiotherapy exam in Saudi Arabia; the DHP nurse, pharmacist (NPQE), physiotherapist and lab technologist exams in Qatar; and the DHA Registered Nurse, Pharmacist, Physiotherapist and Medical Laboratory Technologist exams in Dubai.

Are these real exam questions?

No. The questions are written for practice in the style of each exam, and every answer links to its source.

Is Ijtiaz Med connected to SCFHS, DHP, DHA or Prometric?

No. Ijtiaz Med is an independent study app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any licensing authority or exam provider.

Does Ijtiaz Med have an AI tutor?

Yes. With a free account you can ask the AI tutor about any question, up to 5 messages a day. It answers only from that question's explanation and source, and it can make mistakes, so the explanation and the source stay the reference.

What languages is the app in?

The app works in English and Arabic. The questions are in English, like the real exams.
