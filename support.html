Support

Need help, found a mistake, or want your data? Email ijtiazmed@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

How do I report a wrong question?

Open the question and tap Report. Choose the reason and add a note if you like. Every report is reviewed.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to More → Account → Delete account and confirm. Your account and study data are deleted from our servers straight away. If you can't open the app, email us from the address you signed up with and we will delete it for you.

How do I clear my progress without an account?

Go to More → Reset progress on this device.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Yes, if you sign in. Your progress is saved to your account and appears on any phone where you sign in with the same account.

Are these real exam questions?

No. The questions are written for practice, and each answer links to its official source. Ijtiaz Med is independent and not connected to any licensing authority or exam provider.

Is the app free?

Yes, the app is free at launch.
