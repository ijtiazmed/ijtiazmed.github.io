Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains what Ijtiaz Med collects, why, and what you can do about it. We collect as little as we can: no name, no phone number, no location, no advertising or tracking.

1. Who we are

Ijtiaz Med (“the app”, “we”) is an exam-preparation app for healthcare licensing exams in Saudi Arabia. It is run by Ahmad Ayman Ashour, an individual developer based in Qatar. For any privacy question or request, email ijtiazmed@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

If you use the app without an account, your progress stays on your phone. We do not receive it.

If you create an account, we store:

  • Account details: your email address, or the identifier Apple or Google gives us if you sign in with them. We do not ask for your name, phone number, location or ID.
  • Study activity: the answers you choose, whether they were correct, time spent per question, saved questions, mock exam results, your study plan (exam date, study days, minutes per day) and, if you tell us, whether you passed your exam.
  • Question reports: the reason you choose and any note you write.
  • AI tutor messages (only if the tutor is switched on for you): the messages you send and the replies, linked to the question you asked about.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, such as IP address and time of request, for security and to keep the service running.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell your data.

On your phone only: your language, appearance and reminder settings. Study reminders are created on your phone and are not sent to us.

3. Why we use it

  • To run the app: save your progress, sync it across your devices, and build your study plan and readiness estimate.
  • To keep questions accurate: we review reports, and we compare how all students answer each question to find questions that may be wrong.
  • To improve the readiness estimate: pass/fail answers are used in combined form to check how well the estimate works.
  • To run the AI tutor, if it is switched on for you.
  • To prevent abuse and keep the service secure.

We use your data because you agreed to it when you created your account, and because we need it to provide the service you asked for.

4. Who processes it for us

  • Supabase stores the database and handles sign-in. Its servers for this app are in Frankfurt, Germany (European Union).
  • Apple and Google handle “Sign in with Apple” and “Sign in with Google” if you use them, and the app stores handle downloads and any future payments.
  • Anthropic processes AI tutor messages to write the replies. It receives your message and the question's text, not your email address.
  • Expo delivers app updates and receives basic device and app-version information when it does.

Each of these providers processes data under its own terms. We share data with authorities only when the law requires it.

5. Where your data is stored

Your account data is stored by Supabase in Frankfurt, Germany (European Union), which is outside Saudi Arabia and Qatar. It is encrypted in transit and protected by our providers' security and contractual commitments.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account and study data: until you delete your account.
  • AI tutor messages: 30 days.
  • Question reports: kept so we can keep questions accurate. When you delete your account, they are kept without any link to you.
  • Server logs: a short time (up to 7 days) with our hosting provider.

7. Your rights

You can:

  • See and correct your data: your progress is shown in the app. For a copy, or to correct your email address, email ijtiazmed@gmail.com.
  • Delete your account at any time: in the app, go to More → Account → Delete account. This deletes your account and study data from our servers straight away, and from your phone. Without an account, More → Reset progress on this device clears your phone.
  • Withdraw consent by deleting your account.
  • Complain to the data protection authority: the Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA) in Saudi Arabia, or the National Cyber Security Agency in Qatar.

We reply to requests within 30 days.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Our database only lets each user read their own records, and passwords are stored in hashed form by our sign-in provider. If a data breach is likely to harm you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Age

The app is for adults (18 and over) preparing for professional licensing exams. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and tell you in the app before important changes take effect.

11. Contact
