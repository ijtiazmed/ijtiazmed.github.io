Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

1. Who we are

Ijtiaz Med is an exam-preparation app for healthcare licensing exams in Saudi Arabia, Qatar and Dubai. Ijtiaz Med is operated by its owner, an individual based in the State of Qatar ("we", "us"). You can contact us at ijtiazmed@gmail.com. The operator's legal name is provided on request to any user, court or authority. We decide how your personal data is used, which makes us the controller of that data. Contact: ijtiazmed@gmail.com.

2. The short version

  • We collect what the app needs to work: your email, your study activity, and records that protect our question bank from copying.
  • We do not show ads, we do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not sell your data.
  • Your data is stored in Germany.
  • You can delete your account and its data in the app at any time.

5. Automatic checks

Our servers check automatically for patterns that suggest copying or misuse. Examples are taking a large number of questions without answering them, answering faster than a person can read, reaching the daily limit, or using an account on many devices.

A check can slow an account down for about a week, and for longer if the pattern repeats. It cannot suspend or close an account. Only a person can do that, after looking at the record. If you think a check was wrong, email us and a person will review it.

7. Where your data is stored

Your account data is stored in Germany. Some of the companies above process data in other countries, including the United States. These countries are outside Saudi Arabia, Qatar and the United Arab Emirates.

We send data there because the service cannot run without these providers. By creating an account you agree to this transfer. We use providers that commit in their contracts to protect the data and to use it only for our purposes. If you do not want your data stored outside your country, do not create an account. You can still use the first look and the free practice questions, which stay on your phone.

9. Your rights

You can:

  • see the data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • correct your email address;
  • delete your account and its data, in the app (More, then Account, then Delete account) or by emailing us from the address you signed up with;
  • withdraw your consent, by deleting your account or, for the tutor, by not using it;
  • object to a use of your data, or ask us to limit it;
  • ask for a person to review a decision made by an automatic check.

Email ijtiazmed@gmail.com from your account's address so we can confirm it is you. We reply within 30 days.

10. Complaints

Please contact us first so we can put things right. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live:

  • Saudi Arabia: the Saudi Data and Artificial Intelligence Authority (SDAIA).
  • Qatar: the National Cyber Security Agency, through its National Data Privacy Office.
  • United Arab Emirates: the federal data protection authority (the UAE Data Office, or the authority that takes over its role).

11. Security

Connections to our servers are encrypted. Each account can read only its own records. Passwords are stored in hashed form by the sign-in provider, and we cannot see them. The question bank itself is not readable by the app's general database access.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach is likely to harm you, we will tell you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

12. Age

The app is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

13. If the app moves to a company

We may transfer the app to a company we set up or to a new owner. Your data would move with it and stay covered by this policy. We will tell you in the app or by email before that happens.

14. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we publish the new version with a new date. For an important change we tell you in the app or by email at least 14 days before it takes effect.
